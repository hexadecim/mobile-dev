Platform

Every feature exists to answer one question: are we making money on this?

From the first estimate to the final invoice, Aganya ProfitTrack tracks cost against revenue so profitability stops being a quarterly surprise.

Real-time margin dashboards

Live revenue, cost, and margin by project, client, and portfolio — no waiting for month-end close to know how a project performed.

Time & utilization sync

Two-way sync with Jira, Toggl Track, Harvest, and Clockify pulls logged hours in automatically — nothing to re-enter.

Resource cost allocation

Fully-loaded salary, benefits, overhead, and contractor rates mapped to logged hours, roles, and seniority levels.

Client & project profitability reports

Rank every client and project by margin, spot loss-making engagements early, and export board-ready reports in one click.

Budget vs. actual forecasting

Burn-down tracking against scope and velocity flags fixed-bid projects trending over budget while there's still time to act.

Custom margin alerts

Set thresholds per project or client and get a Slack or email alert the moment margin drifts out of range — not at invoicing.

Fixed-bid vs. T&M comparison

Compare profitability across billing models to see which engagement types, clients, and services actually pay for your growth.

Multi-currency & multi-entity

Built for distributed and offshore delivery teams — track cost and billing across currencies and legal entities in one view.

Finance-ready exports

Push margin and cost data to QuickBooks, Xero, or CSV so finance can reconcile without re-keying anything.
