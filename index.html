FAQ

Common questions

What is Aganya ProfitTrack?
Aganya ProfitTrack is a project profitability management platform built for software development and IT services companies. It connects time tracking, payroll cost, contractor rates, and cloud spend to show you the true margin on every project and client in real time.
How is it different from a time tracker?
Time trackers tell you how many hours were logged. Aganya ProfitTrack converts those hours into fully-loaded cost, compares it against what the client is billed, and shows you the resulting margin per project, sprint, and team member — plus forecasts for where a project is heading.
Does it integrate with the tools we already use?
Yes. Aganya ProfitTrack integrates with Jira, GitHub, Toggl Track, Harvest, Clockify, QuickBooks, Xero, and standard CSV/HR exports, so you don't have to change how your delivery teams work.
Is it suitable for a small development shop?
Yes. The Starter plan is designed for teams with up to 5 active projects, and every plan scales up without a data migration as the studio or agency grows.
