About Aganya Solutions

We build the finance layer that software delivery teams never had

Aganya Solutions was founded by former delivery leads and finance operators from IT services firms who were tired of finding out a project was unprofitable only after it shipped.

Our story

Every software development company tracks hours. Most track revenue. Almost none connect the two in real time, project by project. That gap is where margin quietly disappears — in scope creep, in bench time, in offshore rate mismatches nobody reconciles until the quarter closes.

We started Aganya Solutions to close that gap with a single product: Aganya ProfitTrack, purpose-built for the billing models and delivery patterns of software development and IT services companies — not a generic project management or accounting tool stretched to fit.

Today, delivery leaders and finance teams at development studios, IT consultancies, and staff augmentation firms use Aganya ProfitTrack to see project, client, and team profitability the moment it changes — not weeks later.
