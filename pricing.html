FAQ

Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from account settings, and billing is prorated automatically.
Is there a free trial?
Yes, every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to features on that plan — no credit card required to start.
How is a "user" counted?
A user is anyone who logs into Aganya ProfitTrack directly — typically PMs, delivery leads, and finance. Engineers whose time is synced from Jira or a time tracker are not counted as seats.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing?
Yes, annual billing saves approximately 20% compared to monthly billing on the Starter and Growth plans.
